# Section SFX

Section-specific reveal sounds live here.

- `macros/` - fats, carbs, and protein section scoring or bar-fill sounds
- `microns/` - vitamin/mineral bar confirmations and 100% firework sounds
- `pros/` - major pro reveal accents
- `cons/` - major con reveal accents

Mirror browser-ready files under the same relative path in `docs/audio/sfx/sections/`.
