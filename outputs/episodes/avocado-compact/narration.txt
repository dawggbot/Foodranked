Ranked!

14.7 grams of fat. Polyunsaturated fat is 1.8 grams, helping with cell structure and healthy signalling. Saturated fat is 2.1 grams, working against a cleaner fat profile. For fruit, fat is usually just a small side note.
-
8.5 grams of carbs. Sugar is 0.7 grams, helping keep the sugar load under control. Fibre is 6.7 grams, helping with digestion and steadier meals. For fruit, the carb score mostly comes down to sugar control and fibre.
-
2 grams of protein. That amount is useful enough to count. Bioavailability is 35%, not great for how much protein your body can use. For fruit, protein is usually too low to matter much.
-
Vitamin C is 11% daily value, useful for collagen formation and antioxidant support. Vitamin A is only 2% daily value, not bringing much vision or immune support. For fruit, vitamin C and vitamin A are usually the vitamin scores that matter most.
-
Potassium is 10% daily value, useful for fluid balance. Iron is only 3% daily value, not bringing much oxygen-transport support. For fruit, minerals are a bonus unless the numbers are unusually strong.
-
The upsides first: a true outlier for fruit fat quality. This behaves nothing like typical sweet fruit and that makes the category result exceptional. Easy to use as a whole food. Simple, recognisable, and realistic for everyday eating. Brings a clean fruit identity. It still contributes something valuable even when it is not elite.
-
The drawbacks next: calorie density is much higher than most fruits. Still useful, but it changes how casually you can throw it in. Can be easy to waste if timing is off. Perishability is a practical downside people actually feel. Ripeness window is annoyingly narrow. It can go from not ready to overripe fast.
-
Big strengths are fibre, protein, and a true outlier for fruit fat quality, but the biggest weaknesses are low iron, low vitamin A, and saturated fat. It's good for heart health, digestion, and practical meals because fibre is useful for heart health, fibre helps digestion and keeps meals steadier, and it's easy to build meals around.
