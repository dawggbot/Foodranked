The upsides first: easy to use as a whole food. Simple, recognisable, and realistic for everyday eating. One of the clearest antioxidant-identity fruits. Blueberries have a stronger cultural and practical antioxidant case than a generic fruit baseline. Hydrating whole-fruit package. Water and whole-food structure make it easier to use well than juice or sweets.
-
Cons next: small berries disappear quickly. The handful format can make portions less obvious. Satiety is not especially strong. Fruit often needs help from the rest of the meal. Sugar can climb fast. A lot of fruits look healthy but still lean sweet.
