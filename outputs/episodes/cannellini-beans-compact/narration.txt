Positives first: creamy texture works in proper meals. They blend into soups and sauces while still holding their shape in salads. Cheap dry pantry staple. A bag stores well and turns into several meals for very little money. Mild flavour takes seasoning well. Herbs, tomato, garlic, and stronger sauces all work.
-
Cons next: can be rough on digestion. Like other beans, they can cause gas or bloating for some people. Dry beans take time and planning. Soaking and a full cook make them less convenient than opening a can. They must be cooked properly. Raw or undercooked kidney beans can retain lectins, so this is not a casual low-heat ingredient.

Best bits are protein and fibre. Weak spots are low vitamin C and low zinc. Best for heart health and digestion because fibre is useful for heart health and fibre helps digestion and keeps meals steadier.
