Positives first: extreme water-binding fibre. Chia is unusually good at turning a small amount into a thick, filling gel. ALA omega-3 is the headline fat. The omega-3 number is the food-specific reason chia stands out among seeds. Easy to boost meals invisibly. A small spoonful can disappear into yogurt, oats, or smoothies.
-
Downsides next: works best with enough fluid. Dry chia is not the easiest food unless it is soaked or mixed properly. Phytates add an absorption caveat. The mineral numbers are high, but not all of that is automatically absorbed. Gel texture and digestion can be divisive. The same gel quality that helps satiety can put some people off.
-
Big strengths are omega 3 and protein, but the biggest weaknesses are works best with enough fluid and phytates add an absorption caveat.
