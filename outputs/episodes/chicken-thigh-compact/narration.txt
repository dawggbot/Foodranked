Pros first: dark meat stays flavorful and forgiving to cook. That makes chicken thigh easier to use well in real meals than drier leaner cuts. Usually a reasonably affordable whole-food protein. Chicken thigh is often cheaper than premium animal proteins while still providing a substantial protein serving. Easy to build complete meals around. It works as an actual centrepiece protein rather than a garnish ingredient.
-
Cons next: fattier than lean poultry options like breast meat. That tradeoff is part of why thigh tastes better but is not the cleanest poultry profile. The final health profile changes a lot with cooking method. Breading, deep frying, or skin-on preparation can shift the outcome substantially from this base record. Raw poultry needs careful handling. Food-safety friction is a practical downside versus foods that are safer or easier to store and prep.
