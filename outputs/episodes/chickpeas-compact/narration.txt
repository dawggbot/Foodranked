The upsides first: strong all-round legume profile. Fibre, minerals, and useful protein all show up well enough to make it a very solid category food. Easy to use across lots of meals. Works in bowls, curries, salads, hummus, and snacks. Pretty filling and steady. Useful as a practical staple rather than just a niche health food.
-
Cons next: sugar is a bit high for the category. Not a disaster, but it is less clean than the best legumes on this axis. Can be rough on some people digestively. Legume tolerance varies a lot. Contains some anti-nutrients. Phytates may reduce mineral uptake somewhat.

Big strengths are protein, omega 3, and fibre, but the biggest weaknesses are low vitamin E, low potassium, and sugar is a bit high for the category.
