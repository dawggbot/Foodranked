Cons next: quality drops with heat, light, and age. The best part of extra virgin olive oil depends on freshness and storage. Extremely energy dense. It is still pure fat, so the calories arrive fast. Easy to overpour without noticing. Free-pouring can quietly add a lot more than intended.
