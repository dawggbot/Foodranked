Flax Seeds!
-
Ranked!
-
42.2 grams of fat. Omega 3 is 22813 milligrams, supporting a more useful fat profile. Monounsaturated fat is 7.5 grams, not adding much monounsaturated-fat support, so that's a real downside for seeds. For seeds, fat quality is a big reason to use them.
-
28.9 grams of carbs. Fibre is 27.3 grams, helping with digestion and steadier meals. Starch is 1.3 grams, not bringing much steady carb energy, so that's a small downside for seeds. For seeds, fibre matters much more than raw carb totals.
-
18.3 grams of protein. Essential amino acids score is 9 out of 9, so every essential group clears FoodRanked's useful-amount threshold. Protein digestibility is about 92%, meaning most of that protein can be digested and absorbed, making its amino acids available for the body to use. Lysine is still the limiting amino acid, so highly digestible does not mean perfectly balanced. For seeds, that makes flax strong support protein rather than a perfect standalone source.
-
Vitamins are low overall. For seeds, vitamin E matters most.
-
Magnesium is 93% daily value, adding useful mineral support. Calcium is 20% daily value, not bringing much bone support. For seeds, minerals can be a main strength.
-
Pros first: one of the richest lignan food sources. Flax is unusually concentrated in plant lignans, which gut microbes can convert into enterolignans. Ground seed works as an egg-free binder. Mixed with water, ground flax forms a gel that can bind simple batters and bakes. Easy shelf-stable meal add-on. Ground flax can disappear into oats, yogurt, smoothies, and dough without needing a separate meal.
-
Cons next: whole seeds leave more nutrition locked away. Human crossover research found milling substantially improved access to flax lignans compared with intact seeds. Large amounts can feel rough. Large amounts can cause bloating, fullness, or diarrhoea, especially when intake rises quickly. 100 grams is far beyond a normal serving. The comparison basis is useful for ranking, but a spoonful delivers only a fraction of the displayed totals.
-
Best bits are protein and omega 3. Weak spots are low vitamin E and low calcium. Best for heart health and hormone health because fibre is useful for heart health and the fat section has enough useful fats for a support role.
