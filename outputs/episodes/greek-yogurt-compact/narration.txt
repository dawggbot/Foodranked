The upsides first: fermented-food angle adds extra value. Plain Greek yogurt can bring live-culture context that plain milk does not. Thick texture helps satiety. The strained texture makes it more filling than many softer dairy options. Easy unsweetened protein base. It works in meals without needing added sugar.
-
Cons next: dairy tolerance is not universal. Lactose sensitivity or dairy avoidance can make it a poor fit for some people. Sweetened versions change the profile fast. The plain record is strong, but common flavoured tubs can add a lot of sugar. Saturated fat varies by style. Whole-milk versions carry more saturated fat than low-fat or nonfat versions.

Big strengths are omega 3 and protein, but the biggest weaknesses are dairy tolerance is not universal and sweetened versions change the profile fast.
