Oats ranked.

6.9 grams of fat. Cholesterol is 0 milligrams, keeping the fat tradeoff lighter. Omega 3 is 111 milligrams, supporting a more useful fat profile, but it is the softer support mark in this section. Fat is basically not the story here.

66.3 grams of carbs. Glycemic index is in the strongest display band, helping with steadier carb behaviour. Starch is 55 grams, supporting useful staple-carb energy, but it is the softer support mark in this section. For grains, the carb quality matters much more than the raw number.

16.9 grams of protein. Essential amino acids score is 9 out of 9, making the protein useful for repair and maintenance. Bioavailability is 62%, helping more of that protein count, but it is the softer support mark in this section. Protein helps, but not enough if the carb side is weak.

Vitamins are low all round. For grains, vitamins are more of a bonus after carb quality and minerals, and this section is not adding much.

Magnesium is 44% daily value, adding useful mineral support. Calcium is only 5% daily value, weak for bone support, which is a weak mark for grains.

Pros first: contains meaningful beta-glucan soluble fibre. Oats are unusual among common grains for their beta-glucan content, which makes the fibre story more specific than just the fibre grams already shown. Easy whole-grain staple to use regularly. Plain rolled oats are cheap, familiar, and realistic for repeated use, which matters for an everyday grain. Plain oats start unsweetened. That gives them a cleaner baseline than sweetened instant oatmeal products, which are excluded from this entry.

Downsides next: phytates can modestly reduce mineral uptake. That caveat is real for oats, even if it does not erase their mineral content. The bowl can get worse fast with sugary add-ons. The plain ingredient is solid, but common preparation choices can change the real meal a lot. Texture and tolerance are not universal wins. Some people dislike the texture or do not tolerate oats especially well, which is a real usability downside for a staple food.

Big strengths are protein, fibre, and omega 3, but the biggest weaknesses are low vitamin B12, low calcium, and phytates can modestly reduce mineral uptake. It is good for heart health, digestion, and energy and endurance sports because fibre supports the heart-health side, fibre supports digestion and steadier meals, and the carb section gives usable fuel.

S tier.
