Pros first: simple whole-fruit snack. Easy to understand, easy to portion, and useful without much prep. Skin adds colour compounds. Cons next: bruises easily. Soft fruit bruises quickly, so quality can drop fast after transport or storage. Ripeness window is short. The best eating window is narrow, and underripe or overripe plums are much less useful. Pit slows eating down. The stone makes them a little less convenient than fully edible bite-size fruit.
