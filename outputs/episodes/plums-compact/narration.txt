Pros first: easy fresh-fruit snack. It works with no peeling or cooking, so it is easy to use when the fruit is ripe. Peel adds tart colour. Cons next: bruises easily. Soft fruit bruises quickly, so quality can drop fast after transport or storage. Ripeness window is short. The best eating window is narrow, and underripe or overripe plums are much less useful. Stone interrupts snacking. The pit makes them less convenient than fully edible bite-size fruit like berries or grapes.
