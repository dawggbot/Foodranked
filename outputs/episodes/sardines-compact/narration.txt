Sardines!

Ranked!
-
11.5 grams of fat. Omega 3 is 1480 milligrams, supporting a more useful fat profile. Cholesterol is 142 milligrams, adding to the cardiovascular tradeoff, and for meats, fat quality is a major tradeoff. For meats, fat quality matters a lot once the protein is already there.
-
No defensible carb submacros to call out. In this category, carbs barely matter.
-
24.6 grams of protein. Essential amino acids score is 9 out of 9, making the protein useful for repair and maintenance. Collagen is 1.5 grams, weak for connective-tissue protein, so for meats, it is not bringing much connective-tissue protein. Strong protein is expected here, so the rest of the profile decides how high it climbs.
-
Vitamin B12 is 370% daily value, useful for nerve and blood-cell support. Vitamin D is 24% daily value, weak for bone and immune support, which is a small miss for meats.
-
Iron is 16% daily value, useful for oxygen transport. Potassium is 8% daily value, weak for fluid balance, which is a weak mark for meats.
-
The upsides first: small-fish nutrient density is elite. Omega-3s, protein, and micronutrients all come together unusually well here. Easy to centre a meal around. Practical meal value matters. Gives a straightforward protein solution. The category often wins on simplicity and reliability.
-
The drawbacks next: cost and storage can be a pain. Practical downsides still count. Fat quality can go sideways fast. Some meats are strong until the fat profile shows up. Quality swings a lot by cut and processing. The category can look very different depending on what version you actually buy.
-
Big strengths are omega 3, protein, and vitamin B12, but the biggest weaknesses are low potassium, cost and storage can be a pain, and fat quality can go sideways fast. It is good for blood and oxygen support, muscles and strength sports, and hormone health because vitamin B12 and iron supports the blood-and-oxygen side, the protein section supports repair and maintenance, and the fat section has enough useful fats for a support role.
-
S tier.
