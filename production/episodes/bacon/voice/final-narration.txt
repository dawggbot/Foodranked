Pros first: highly palatable and appealing. It is an obvious reason people like it. Works as a strong flavour booster. A small amount can carry a whole plate. Has strong comfort-food appeal. It is instantly recognisable and easy to package in meals.
-
The drawbacks next: sodium is a major drawback. Even a small serving can bring a lot of salt, so it adds up fast. Processing is a major negative. Because bacon is cured meat, the health tradeoff is bigger than the macro numbers alone suggest. Salty, fatty flavour makes portions easy to overdo. That flavour is part of the appeal, but it also makes bacon harder to keep as a small add-on.

Big strengths are protein, palatable and appealing, and strong flavour booster, but the biggest weaknesses are low iron, low vitamin D, and saturated fat.
