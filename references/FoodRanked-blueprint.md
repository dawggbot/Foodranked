# format

* Short form videos, nutrition for general health, fitness, and longevity 
* Only cover wholefoods for now (rice, apples, chicken thigh), not processed, multiple ingredient foods (donuts, crisps, chocolate bars) 
* All foods ranked off per 100g (most familiar format) 
* Narration and word highlighting subtitles for retention 
* Cozy and cheery background pixel / 8 bit music 
* Use food image pixel art as cover image 
* Blurred background for opening sequence 
* “SUBJECT ranked” (opening and attention hook) - big image of SUBJECT (the food image) and ‘RANKED’ that flies in, then flies out when narration is finished. 
* Blur into blank background ready for section 1 
* Permanent header that stays constant throughout video (even on empty background frames), lists key info like food name and food image and calories etc almost like the character info in a character stat sheet 
* 11 types of food, each type has a specific colour palette that will also translate to the display 
* Everything revealed as narration lists them off 
* 7 sections - fats + fat submicros, carbs + carb submicros, proteins + protein submicros, vitamins, minerals, pros, cons. 
* 7 small dots near the bottom, displaying what section we are on by highlighting the corresponding dot. 
* Empty background like start of video 
* Brief summary of strengths, weakness and specific uses 
* “S-D tier” (ending hook for loyal viewers, rank food on a scale of S,A,B,C,D) - big letter that gets stamped in on empty background below the permanent header
* End of video 
* Overall all aesthetics will be like a pixel rpg game stat sheet or a pokemon entry from a pokedex. A kind of cozy atmosphere, but also aesthetically pleasing. My own animated sprites created in aseprite will be used. 
* This format is locked for all videos

D=purple 
C=green 
B=red 
A=light blue 
S=platinum,gold

# food types and rulesets

**Universal rules (red = bad, green = good)**

* Saturated fat: ↑ = RED ↓ = GREEN 
* Omega 3: ↑ = GREEN↓ = RED 
* Poly unsaturated fat: ↑ = GREEN ↓ = RED 
* Cholesterol: ↑ = RED ↓ = GREEN 
* Starch: ↑ = GREEN ↓ = RED 
* Fiber: ↑ = GREEN ↓ = RED 
* Sugar: ↑ = RED ↓ = GREEN 
* Glycemic Index: ↑ = RED ↓ = GREEN 
* Collagen: ↑ = GREEN ↓ = RED 
* Essential amino acids: ↑ = GREEN ↓ = RED 
* Non-essential amino acids: ↑ = GREEN ↓ = RED 
* Bioavailability: ↑ = GREEN ↓ = RED

**Colour palette** 

* Vegetables — Leaf Green (#4CAF50) 
 Plants, chlorophyll, obvious connection. Green instantly signals “veg”. 
* Fruits — Bright Red (#E53935) 
 Fruit colours vary, but red reads as sweet and vibrant. 
* Grains — Golden Wheat (#D4A017) 
 Think wheat fields and bread crust. 
* Legumes — Earth Brown (#8D6E63) 
 Beans and lentils tend to sit in earthy tones. 
* Tubers — Rust Orange (#BF6D2A) 
 Potatoes, sweet potatoes, carrots—root-vegetable territory. 
* Nuts — Walnut Brown (#6D4C41) 
 A darker woody brown fits well. 
* Seeds — Sand Beige (#C2A878) 
 Lighter and drier than nuts. 
* Meats — Deep Red (#8B0000) 
 Classic butcher-shop red. 
* Dairy — Cream White (#F5F5DC) 
 Milk, yogurt, cheese—light and soft. 
* Oils & Fats — Amber Yellow (#FFC107) 
 Looks like olive oil or melted butter. 
* Misc — Neutral Grey (#9E9E9E) 
 Anything uncategorizable lives in neutral space.

# section rules

**Universal**

* Sections layout and rules are locked for every food video

**Section 1 - section 3 (fats, carbs, proteins)**

* Same layout for all three 
* Macros will be main appeal and will appear first 
* Big bubble with icon for each macro 
* Big bar for all macros coming out of its macro bubble that fills out based on value 
* Then all submacros appear below macro bubble and bar 
* Arrows in groups of 1 to 3 next to submacro values to tell you if it is really high/low (3 arrows) high/low (2 arrows) or a little high/low (1 arrow). The direction of the arrow tells you if it's high/low obviously with pointing up being high and pointing down being low. The colour of the arrow (red/green) will show whether being high/low is good/bad, obviously with red being bad and green being good. 

**Section 4 - section 5 (vitamins and minerals)**

* Same layout for both 
* Bar graphs scaling upwards 
* Display entire bar graphs all at once to reduce unnecessary video length and narration. 
* Bars will be on DV%, a maxed out bar means per 100g of this food gives you your full daily value in whatever that bar is 
* Abbreviations might be needed (vitA - Vitamin A, Fe - iron) 
* Colours assigned to each vitamin and mineral, in a relatively fitting manner (iron being a rusty brown/maroon… etc)

**Section 6 - section 7 (pros and cons)**

* Same layout for both 
* In bullet point format, revealed one at a time 
* Have indicator (maybe in the bullet point) showing whether these pros/cons are either major/minor 
* include extra info not related to the nutrition profiles listed in previous sections (unless it adds extra to them like iron being super absorbable heme iron) E.G. vasodilation effects, sodium levels, antioxidants, polyphenols, pesticides from agricultural factors… etc) 

# ranking

* Macros don't get scored, they are entirely visual; they are there to inform the viewer of their amounts
* submacros, scores based on what arrow indicator is displayed 
* Vitamins and minerals get scored off what bar % is filled. 
* Major pros give more points than minor
* Major cons deduct more points than minor 
* All these score metrics are locked and universal, what changes them slightly would be food type weighting 
* Food types get recognised for what they are strong in. More score weighting in certain areas. Would save stuff like tubers getting penalised for having 0.9g of saturated fat. However this also gives more penalisation for underperforming. 
* Let AI sort out tier thresholds?
* Misc submacros don't get ranked; vitamins, minerals, pros, and cons get scored heavier instead.
* None of the scoring will be displayed on the video, only the tier 
* All sections contribute towards the score fairly
